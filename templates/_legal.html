Privacy notice

Last updated: October 28, 2026

JZH Land is a small, non-commercial website for a group of friends. This notice explains what the site stores and how it is used.

What the site stores

  • Your account: your username and a hashed version of your password. Nobody, including the site admin, can see your actual password.
  • What you post: posts, comments, uploaded images and messages left for Lao Jiang. If you post anonymously, other users won't see your name, but the server still records who posted it.
  • Game data: cards, matches, pets, coins, scores and similar progress.
  • Your chat history with JZH, so she can remember the conversation.

What is sent to third parties

  • JZH's replies are generated by third-party AI services (currently DeepSeek, with Google Gemini as a backup). When you chat with JZH, mention @JZH on the board, or she replies to a post on her own, the related text (and your username, unless the post is anonymous) is sent to these services. Please don't write real names, contact details or other personal information there.
  • Apart from this, the site does not sell, rent or share your information. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

Cookies and local storage

  • A login cookie (required) and a language cookie that remembers whether you chose 中文 or English.
  • Your board character settings are saved in your own browser's local storage and are never uploaded.

Server logs

The server may log the time and IP address of requests. These logs are only used to fix problems and prevent abuse.

Deleting your data

To delete your account or anything you posted, contact the site admin.

JZH乐园是朋友之间的非商业小网站。这里说明本站会保存哪些信息，以及怎么使用。

本站保存的信息

  • 账号：用户名，以及经过哈希处理的密码。包括站长在内，任何人都看不到你的密码原文。
  • 你发布的内容：帖子、评论、上传的图片、给老江的留言。选择匿名发帖时，其他用户看不到你的名字，但服务器仍会记录是谁发的。

会发送给第三方的信息

  • JZH 的回复由第三方 AI 服务生成（目前是 DeepSeek，备用 Google Gemini）。你和 JZH 聊天、在留言板 @JZH，或者她自己回复帖子时，相关文字（以及非匿名时的用户名）会发送给这些服务。请不要在这些地方写真实姓名、联系方式等个人信息。
  • 除此之外，本站不出售、不出租，也不向其他人提供你的信息。网站没有广告，也没有第三方统计。

Cookie 和本地存储

  • 登录状态 cookie（必需），以及记住你选择中文还是 English 的语言 cookie。
  • 留言板的立绘设置保存在你自己浏览器的本地存储里，不会上传。

服务器日志

服务器可能记录访问时间和 IP 地址，只用于排查故障和防止滥用。
